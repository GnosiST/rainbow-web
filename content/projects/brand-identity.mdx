# Brand Identity System

A complete brand identity system for a creative agency, including logo, color palette, typography, and brand guidelines.

## Scope

- Logo design and variations
- Color palette definition
- Typography selection
- Brand guidelines document
- Stationery design

## Concept

The brand identity reflects the agency's innovative and creative spirit through bold colors and dynamic shapes.

## Deliverables

- Primary and secondary logos
- Brand color palette (primary, secondary, accent)
- Typography guidelines
- 50+ page brand guidelines PDF
- Business card and letterhead designs
