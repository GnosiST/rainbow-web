# Illustration Series

A series of editorial illustrations created for a publishing house's quarterly magazine.

## Project Overview

Created 12 unique illustrations for the magazine's feature articles, covering topics from technology to lifestyle.

## Style

The illustrations feature a modern, minimalist style with bold colors and clean lines, designed to complement the magazine's contemporary aesthetic.

## Process

1. Concept sketches based on article themes
2. Color palette development
3. Digital illustration in Procreate
4. Final refinements and delivery
